Try it (about 2 minutes)

  1. Pick #102 Mobile app launch: it has no saved copy, so the template loads.
  2. Change a Likelihood or Impact. Scores, ratings and the overall rating recalculate.
  3. Click Save workbook. A copy is attached to this work item and the RAG and Risk score fields on Details update.
  4. Open ⚙ Extension settings, upload a new template version, then come back: #102 keeps its own copy; a new work item gets the new template.
  5. Try Simulate another person saved, then Save.

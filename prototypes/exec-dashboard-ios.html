Device

Executive: iOS redesign (prototype)

Built from scratch around one question: “Where do I need to step in today?” Nothing in the real extension has been changed.

Structure

  • Overview: health, status, what needs you, next go-lives, delivery performance
  • Pipeline: the delivery stages and the bottleneck
  • Epics: search, filter, grouped by status
  • Insights: by-field bars or table, go-live by quarter, delivery

HIG choices

  • Large titles that collapse into a blurred bar on scroll
  • Inset grouped lists and cards, 16 pt margins, 17 pt body, Dynamic-Type-friendly sizes
  • Tab bar on iPhone, sidebar (NavigationSplitView) on iPad
  • Detail as a sheet with a grabber (iPad: side panel); drag down to dismiss
  • Status is never colour only: icon plus word
  • Light appearance only; burgundy as the single accent
  • Reduced motion respected

Haptics (the pill at the top)

  • Tab and chip change: .selection
  • Open or close a sheet: .impact(.light)
  • Refresh done: .success

SwiftUI mapping

  • @Observable final class DashboardModel, loaded with async/await and .refreshable
  • TabView / NavigationSplitView, NavigationStack, .sheet with presentationDetents([.medium,.large])
  • Swift Charts: BarMark stacked by status, SectorMark for rings
  • .sensoryFeedback(.selection, trigger:)
  • SF Symbols: chart.bar.xaxis, arrow.triangle.branch, list.bullet, chart.line.uptrend.xyaxis, exclamationmark.triangle.fill, checkmark.circle.fill

# Resource Planner & Heatmap

See who works on which Epic and how each person's capacity is used – a **utilization heatmap**, a **resource timeline** and an **Epic Gantt** for Azure Boards. Read-only: nothing is edited from the planner.

> Work in progress – not yet released.

Hours come from the Resource Allocation grids of your Epics (Work Item Grid & Rules); available capacity comes from Azure DevOps team capacity.

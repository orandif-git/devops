# Changelog

## Resource Planner & Heatmap (separate extension, `planner/`)
- Executive Dashboard: go-live, start and last-changed dates, the go-live history, the query date filters and "today" are read in the viewer's own time zone, like the stage dates, so a go-live set as 17 Dec 00:00 shows as 17 Dec (it showed the 16th in a time zone ahead of UTC). The cached go-live history is read again once, because it held the UTC day.
- **Stage dates card in the Classic Report** (one shared card for both designs, Classic Report and Modern Overview): the number of Epics that have **stage dates to check** and the number that **had a stage date changed**; a click filters the dashboard. “To check” is the data problems found in the stage dates: a start date missing, dates out of order, a skipped stage, or an Epic back in an earlier stage (rework); the card breaks the count down by kind and says these are records to look at, not always mistakes (a skipped stage or rework can be real). It is shown only when the stage dates are set up. The wording changed from “dates that do not fit” to “stage dates to check”.
- **Stage dates on the Modern Overview**: the **Stage dates** filter is now pinned in the Modern filter bar (it was under More filters), so it is there on the Overview and every other page, and the Overview has a **Stage dates** card with the number of Epics whose dates do not fit and whose dates were changed; a click on either filters the whole dashboard (click again to clear).
- **Stage dates filter in Classic, and the stage on the timelines**: the Classic filters (the filter row and the Portfolio map rail) now have **Stage dates** (Dates changed, Date problems, with counts) when the stage dates are set up. The stage is on the timelines: in the Classic **Roadmap** each Epic row says “state · stage” and the bar carries the stage name when it is wide enough (the tooltip has it too); in the Modern **Go-live timeline** each dot holds the number of its stage, the name is in the tooltip and a key under the chart lists the numbers. Also fixed a raw “\u2019” in the Roadmap legend.
- **Stage dates in the Classic Epic panel too**: the Classic panel now shows the same stage dates as the Modern one (how long each stage took, the dates with what each was derived from, the flags, the typical durations and the history of the stage dates with “recorded late”), and a **Stage** line with the days in it. Both panels use the same component, so they always agree. Nothing changes in Classic while the stage dates are off.
- **Stage dates** (Settings › Executive Dashboard › Delivery stages and progress › Stage dates, optional, off by default): map the date fields that say when each stage started and ended. Each stage has **Starts when** (a date field, or “when the previous stage ends”) and **Ends when** (its own field, **when the next stage starts**, or open), so a stage with no “completed” field, for example Development, simply ends when SIT starts. What it gives:
  - **Time in the current stage** and **how long each finished stage took** from the dates (the time in stage and the stuck rule now use them first, the history second), and a **delivered date** field for on-time delivery and days late;
  - the **Epic panel** (Modern design) shows the dates under each stage, a bar of how long each stage took, a table with started, ended and duration (marked “= SIT start” where an end is the next start, “from history” where a date was missing, and how it compares with the **typical** duration of that stage across your Epics), and the **history of the stage dates**: who changed a date, from what to what, and when, with **“recorded late”** when a date was entered more than N days after the date itself;
  - **problems are found, not guessed**: a missing start date (read from the history when that option is on), dates out of order (no duration shown), a skipped stage, and rework (the stage field is behind the dates; time counts from when the field changed), each with a sentence in the panel; the Data notes line counts them, and under More filters (Modern design) **Stage dates** filters by “Dates changed” and “Date problems”;
  - changes to the dates are tracked from the work item history like the go-live date changes (it needs the history to be readable; the first entry and who made it are kept too).
- **Modern design corrections**: the **Epics from** (and Saved filter) choices are now in the filter bar; the Status from switch has **Owner’s RAG** and **Data** only (Variance stays in the Classic design; a person who left Classic on Variance gets Owner’s RAG). The Classic | Modern switch in the Classic toolbar now has two equal buttons (it had room for three). “trend starts next week” now reads **trend appears next week**, with a tooltip: the trend compares this week with last week, and the first week has nothing to compare with.
- **Modern design for the Executive Dashboard (additional, switchable)**: a new desktop design next to the existing one, which is not changed. Settings › Executive Dashboard › Dashboard › **Dashboard design**: turn on **Classic**, **Modern** or both (at least one is always on; Modern is off by default, so existing setups look exactly as before), and choose which one people **start with**. With only one on, everyone gets that one. With both on, a **Classic | Modern** switch appears in the toolbar of both designs and each person's choice is remembered for them (in their saved-views document). The Modern design reads the same data and has the same filters, saved views, saved filters, Epic queries, full screen, refresh and Epic details:
  - a **toolbar** (page tabs Overview / Pipeline / Epics / Insights, search with the `/` shortcut, Views, full screen, refresh) and a **filter bar** (Status, Stage and the first category field pinned, **More filters** for the rest with counts, the Period filter, and the Owner’s RAG / Data / Variance switch where it applies), with removable filter chips;
  - **Overview**: portfolio health with its trend, a card per status (click to filter), “Needs your attention”, the delivery pipeline, delivery performance, a **go-live timeline** for the next six months, the By-field chart and go-live by quarter;
  - **Pipeline**, **Epics** (sortable table, calculated fields as extra columns, arrow keys move) and **Insights** (By-field, delivery performance, go-live by quarter, go-live changes);
  - an **Epic panel** on the right (stage stepper with where it should be, progress, go-live and forecast, why the status is what it is, go-live history) with **Open Epic** (opens the work item) and **Epic details**.
- **Delivery stages setup made simple** (Settings › Executive Dashboard › Delivery stages and progress): three short steps like the prototype. 1) Where the stage comes from: **A field / The state / Field, then the state** and the delivery-phase field. 2) The **stages as one comma list** (Design, Development, SIT, UAT, Deployment), with a **Use the SDLC stages** quick start, and a small table of what each stage counts as and its planned window. 3) **Which value is which stage**: the values of the field’s pick-list (or the states) are listed with a drop-down each, and **Suggest mapping** fills the unmapped ones by name and common words (Doing → Development, Delivery Committed → UAT, Closed and On Hold → ignore); existing choices are kept. Renaming the stages keeps their numbers and mapping, and removing one drops only its mappings. If the chosen field holds Red / Amber / Green, the page says it looks like a RAG status and points to the phase field. The old “Add a stage” rows are gone. Each rule has a one-line example under it.
- **Executive Dashboard polish**: the numbers in the Go-live columns no longer get clipped (columns are wider, and an inner number or ✓ is shown only where it fits); the status donut is larger; the By-field table shows whole rows (eight, with a soft fade when there are more) and its rows are keyboard-focusable; in the Delivery pipeline the Epics that are ignored, not mapped or without a stage are now links that filter to them (“Stage: Not mapped”); on narrow screens the pipeline wraps, the By-field card uses the room it has, and the page no longer scrolls sideways.
- **By-field chart** (Executive Dashboard report, top right): the “By …” card has an **Auto / Bars / Table** switch. **Auto** shows bars up to 12 values (in natural order for an ordered field such as P1, P2… or High / Medium / Low, otherwise biggest first, with the smaller values in an “Other” row and a “Show all” button) and a **sortable table** above that (value, Epics, status mix, a count per status, % at risk, average progress, with a search box and a sticky header). Few values are spread evenly over the card. A click on a bar or table row filters the dashboard. The card has a fixed height (the table and long lists scroll inside it), so the other charts never move. Each category field can have its own default chart in Settings › Executive Dashboard › Epics, grouping and dates; the choice a person makes on the dashboard is kept in their saved views and shared links. (Columns and Pie were tried and removed; a saved Columns or Pie opens as Auto.)
- **Delivery stages are now the core of progress and status** (Executive Dashboard): Settings › Executive Dashboard › Delivery stages and progress. Choose where the stage is read from (a field such as Delivery Status, the state, or the field with the state as fallback), define the stages in order (for example Design, Development, SIT, UAT, Deployment) with what each counts as in percent and its planned window as a share of the Epic's time, and map each value found to a stage (or “(ignore)”). ## 2.2.0 (next)
**Rows & access, and asking before deleting**
- New **Rows & access** part in each grid's settings (all optional; a grid without them behaves as before): **Starting rows** (load a chosen preset automatically into every work item whose grid is empty, new or existing, once, when it opens), **People can** (add, delete, move rows, import or load a preset: switch each off to keep the rows as they are so people only fill in cells), **Fixed cells** (never, cells the preset filled, or any cell once it has a saved value; blank cells always stay editable), **Row owner** (only the person who added a row and administrators can change or delete it; rows with no owner stay open to everyone; others see “Added by …” and cannot change the row), **Administrators** (project administrators plus chosen teams and people always can) and **Ask before deleting a row**.
- Deleting a row on the work item now asks first (a small pop-up on the row: “Delete “Design review”? Cancel / Delete”). Deleting a grid, a preset or a column in the settings asks first too, naming what goes and what uses it (derived grids, calculations, totals, formulas…).
- The figures drawn under a grid (“Sum of Score 16” and the grouped totals) can be hidden: one switch for the whole grid (Totals and fields › “Show the totals and grouped totals under the grid”) and a “Show under the grid” tick on each total and breakdown. Hidden figures are still calculated and written to their fields.
- Fixed cells and the row owner rule are bypassed by administrators (project administrators and the ones listed); a tick, “Administrators can change fixed cells and other people’s rows”, turns that off so the rules apply to everyone, which is also how to test them without signing in as someone else.
- Row owner, fixed rows and fixed cells are guards inside the grid, listed as such on the Security tab: the rows are one JSON value, so someone who edits the field by other means is not stopped. Who saved the field is in the work item’s History.

**Delivery progress (new, off by default)**
- **Delivery Progress** (new, off by default): a read-only delivery bar as the first group of the Epic form. A chunky track of the stages with a round marker where the work item is (its icon follows the stage: code, puzzle piece, clipboard, cloud, rocket), a speech bubble that says it in one line (“On track · SIT starts in 29 days”, “Needs attention · one stage behind schedule”, “At risk · 3 stages behind schedule”, “Delivered · 19 days before go-live”), a dashed “Should be here” mark under the stage the dates say it should be in, a finish flag, and two quiet lines (“In Development since 27 Oct (7 days)”, “Go-live 20 Jan 2027 · 78 days left”). Green on track, amber for one stage behind the dates or a stage past its planned end, red for two stages or more behind, 14 days or more past a planned end, or a passed go-live. It never makes the work item unsaved.
- New **Delivery progress** tab in the settings: one switch adds or removes the whole group in the Epic layout through the process API, so when it is off nothing is shown (no heading, no message). Works on inherited processes only; the manual layout-editor steps are shown for anything else.
